# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Business owners and decision-makers in clinics, local businesses, online brands, and organizations with manual operations. Spanish and English readers need to understand the offer and inspect credible work before starting a project conversation.

## Product Purpose

NullRaccoon's commercial website introduces its web and digital systems services, demonstrates delivery through client projects and clearly labeled demos, and generates qualified inquiries through WhatsApp.

## Brand Personality

Practical, confident, approachable. NullRaccoon is a boutique software business in Tijuana, not a student portfolio. Use human language and explain the business outcome before the implementation.

## Anti-references

Dense text grids, repeated purple buttons, unsupported metrics, fake testimonials, certifications as commercial proof, oversized trust boxes, and undifferentiated template layouts. Never name the company NullRaccoon Studio.

## Design Principles

- Give each page a distinct job: Home orients, Services helps buyers choose, Projects provides proof.
- Show real work, then explain the useful change it enables.
- Keep copy short and actions explicit about internal pages versus external demos.
- Preserve the dark studio identity; demo shops can have their own visual system.
- Distinguish documented delivery, pilot status, and measured results.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support Spanish and English, keyboard navigation, visible focus, mobile layouts, and the shared reduced-motion setting. Contact controls must not cover content.
